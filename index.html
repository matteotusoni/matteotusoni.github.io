About Me

Hi! I’m Matteo Tusoni, a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Sapienza University of Rome.

My research focuses on developing AI methods for understanding complex systems. I work across machine learning, causal discovery, time series analysis, and agent-based modelling.

I currently apply these ideas to financial and macroeconomic systems.
