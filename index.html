About Me

Background & focus

A theoretical physicist turned computer scientist, working at the intersection of causality and finance.

Bio

I am Matteo Tusoni, a PhD researcher in Computer Science at Sapienza University of Rome. My background is in theoretical physics, and my current work focuses on causal inference, financial time series, and rigorous methods for understanding complex systems in macroeconomics and finance.

Current focus

Developing advanced AI methodologies to uncover causality in time series data, with particular attention to financial markets and economic systems. Developing agentic AI simulations for financial markets and new agentic AI techniques to emulate real data scenarios.
